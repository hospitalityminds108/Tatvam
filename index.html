The Five Fundamentals

The fundamentals every home is owed.

These five shape how we actually make development decisions — not a marketing list, but the things a home should give back.

Light

Every home's first right.

Light is something every home owes its residents, not something they should pay extra for. We plan for daylight before we plan for saleable area.

Air

Why a home feels alive.

Ventilation isn't a specification to tick. It's why a home feels alive, not just built. Cross-ventilation and airflow shape our layouts from the first sketch.

Space

Space that improves living.

Space should improve how people live, not simply increase saleable area. We design for how a room is used through the day, not just how it measures.

Community

One connected experience.

Every space should contribute to one connected living experience, not exist in isolation. Shared spaces are designed to be used, not just photographed.

Sustainability

How effortless it feels.

Sustainability isn't a lobby feature. It's how effortless the whole building feels, every day — in water, energy, maintenance and the cost of living there over time.

Our Approach

How we think about development.

We don't start with a building. We start with behaviour — and work back to the structure that serves it.

01

Redevelopment

Redevelopment that starts with understanding.

Mumbai's older housing societies carry decades of life. Redevelopment is how that life continues — with better structure, more space and transparency through the process.

We work across housing society redevelopment, rehabilitation and SRA where applicable — with one clear, authoritative process covering planning, stakeholder considerations, design, execution and long-term living.

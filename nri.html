Frequently asked

NRI questions, answered directly.

Can an NRI book a Tat:vm property from abroad?

Yes. NRIs can book from abroad with documentation, payment through permitted banking channels and a Power of Attorney where required. We assist at each step.

What documents does an NRI need to purchase property in Mumbai?

Typically a PAN, OCI/PIO card or Indian passport, overseas address proof, photographs and a Power of Attorney if you cannot be physically present. Specifics depend on your residency status and the transaction.

Does Tat:vm help with FEMA and tax compliance?

We assist with documentation and guide you to qualified professionals for FEMA, repatriation and tax advice. We do not provide legal or tax advice directly.

Can payments be made from an overseas bank account?

Yes, through permitted banking channels such as NRE/NRO accounts and inward remittances, in line with applicable regulations.

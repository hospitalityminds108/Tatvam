Project Overview

Commercial and retail, designed around movement.

Ghatkopar is a connected, lived-in part of Mumbai's eastern suburbs. This mixed-use development brings commercial and retail spaces together in a way that follows how people actually move through the area — by road, by rail and on foot.

Concept

Built around how the eastern suburbs live.

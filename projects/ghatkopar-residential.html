Project Overview

Homes, shaped around daily living.

Ghatkopar is a connected, lived-in part of Mumbai's eastern suburbs — a neighbourhood with its own rhythm. This residential development is designed around how families actually live in that rhythm: how they move through the day, where they gather, how they rest, and how the building supports it.

Concept

Designed around how the eastern suburbs live.

The concept follows the Tat:vm approach: understand how people live first, then design around it. Daylight paths, airflow, sightlines and shared spaces are resolved before saleable area is optimised. The result is homes that work for the way residents actually use them — not just how they photograph.

The five fundamentals — Light, Air, Space, Community and Sustainability — guide every layout decision. Where daylight enters, how air moves through, where neighbours naturally meet, and how the building ages are all considered from the start.

About Tat:vm

Places shaped around how people live.

Tat:vm is a Mumbai-based real estate development company focused on residential, commercial and mixed-use spaces — and on the details that shape everyday life.

Who We Are

A development company, not a marketing one.

Tat:vm is a Mumbai-based real estate development company focused on residential, commercial and mixed-use spaces. Led by Raahul Maroo, Tat:vm brings 20+ years of Mumbai real estate experience to a new generation of development — with a focus on thoughtful design, responsible execution and the details that shape everyday life.

We define Tat:vm by what it stands for, not by what competitors fail to do. The work is specific, the claims are answerable, and the details can be verified.

Founding Insight

The Mumbai observation behind Tat:vm.

People in Mumbai spend significant time in traffic and at work. A home, then, should give something back — not just shelter, but light, air, space and a sense that someone considered how it would actually be lived in.

Time given to the city

Mumbaikars give hours each day to commute and work. The home is what they return to.

A home should give back

If the city takes time, the home should return ease, comfort and considered design.

Details that shape daily life

It's the small, repeated things — light, air, space — that decide how a home feels over years.

What Tat:vm Means

From Tattvam — truth, reality, essential nature.

The name Tat:vm comes from the Sanskrit word Tattvam — the idea of truth, reality and essential nature. It was chosen because it speaks to building spaces rooted in what genuinely matters, rather than what is merely impressive.

That idea runs through how we work: we begin with the fundamentals of good living and design outward from there.

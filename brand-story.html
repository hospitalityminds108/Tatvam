Brand Story

What Tat:vm means.

An honest explanation of the company — not a poem.

01 — What Tat:vm means

From Tattvam — truth, reality, essential nature.

The name Tat:vm comes from the Sanskrit word Tattvam — the idea of truth, reality and essential nature. It points to the essential nature of a thing: what it actually is, beneath what it appears to be.

For us, that idea is a working principle. A building has an essential nature — how it actually serves the people who live and work in it. Tat:vm is the attempt to build around that nature, not around appearance.

◇ ◆ ◇
02 — Why the name was chosen

Because what genuinely matters should come first.

The name was chosen to connect our work to a simple belief: spaces should be rooted in what genuinely matters — light, air, space, community, sustainability, and the ease of daily life — rather than in what is merely impressive.

It's a name that asks a question of every decision: is this essential, or is it decoration?

03 — The founding insight

Mumbai takes time. A home should give it back.

The founding insight came from observing Mumbai: people here spend significant time in traffic and at work. The hours given to the city are hours the home should quietly return.

So a home isn't only shelter. It's the part of the day that should restore — through daylight that reaches every room, air that moves, space that's used well, and a community that holds together. That's the standard a home owes its residents.

04 — The gap Tat:vm addresses

The fundamentals shouldn't be premium extras.

In much of urban living, the basics — light, air, space, community, sustainability — are treated as features to upgrade into, not things every home is owed. The gap Tat:vm addresses is exactly that: making the fundamentals standard, considered and lasting, rather than reserved for the top of a price sheet.

We don't promise what can't be verified. We build, and the details can be checked.

05 — The five fundamentals, in practice

How they shape actual decisions.

Light

Daylight is planned before saleable area.

We resolve how light reaches each home early in design — it's not a by-product of the layout.

Air

Cross-ventilation shapes the plan.

Airflow is designed in, so a home feels alive rather than just enclosed.

Space

Rooms are designed for use, not just measurement.

We design for how a space is lived in through the day.

Community

Shared spaces are meant to be used.

Common areas are designed around real gathering, not just render appeal.

Sustainability

Effortless living, every day.

Sustainability is measured in how effortless the building feels over time — in water, energy, maintenance and running cost — not in a lobby feature.

Our principle

What responsibility means to us.

Tat:vm builds in Mumbai — a city of intense density, inequality and energy. Responsibility here isn't an annual donation line. It's how we think about the communities our buildings sit inside, the people who build them, and the neighbourhoods that shape them.

We focus on areas connected to what we do: housing access, the built environment, and the people who make buildings possible. Where an initiative is confirmed, we say so. Where it is still being formed, we say that too.
